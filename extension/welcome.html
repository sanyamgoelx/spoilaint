How to use it

Just press play

Turn on subtitles in the player as usual. On YouTube and most sites, Spoilaint takes over by itself.

Netflix, Prime, Hotstar

These hide their sound, so click Spoilaint and press Sync to speech once when you start watching. Or press Alt+S.

Feels early or late?

Nudge it while watching with Alt+[ and Alt+]. Size, colour and style are in Spoilaint's menu.

Try it with your own subtitles

Drop in any .srt or .vtt file (and the video, if you like) to watch the reveal.

Open the test page
VLC is set up too: open any film with subtitles and they reveal word by word. Close this tab whenever you like.
