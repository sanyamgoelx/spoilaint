Spoilaint privacy policy

Applies to the Spoilaint browser extension and the Spoilaint app for Windows. Last updated 9 October 2026.

The short version

Spoilaint doesn't collect, store or send any personal data. Everything it does happens on your computer.

What Spoilaint reads, and why

  • Subtitles of the video you're watching: read on the page to show them word by word. They are never sent anywhere.
  • The sound of the video: only while you watch, and only to tell when each subtitle word is spoken.
    • On some sites this needs your permission each time (the "Sync to speech" button or Alt+S).
    • The sound is processed in memory by a speech model that runs on your computer, and is never recorded, saved or uploaded.
  • Your settings (reveal style, delay, text size, colours, sites where it's off): stored by your browser. If you use Chrome sync, your browser syncs them with your Google account like other extension settings.

What Spoilaint never does

  • No accounts, analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies.
  • No servers: Spoilaint has none, and it sends nothing over the internet.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because there is none to share.

Timing reports

If you choose "Save a timing report" (or press Alt+B), Spoilaint saves a file to your Downloads folder. It holds the subtitle timing and the sound levels of the last few minutes, and it only leaves your computer if you send it to someone yourself.

The Windows app

The Spoilaint app reads the movies you open in VLC and their subtitle files on your computer, to time the subtitles. Its results stay in VLC's and Spoilaint's folders on your computer. It doesn't connect to the internet.
