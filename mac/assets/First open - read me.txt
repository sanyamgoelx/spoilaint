Spoilaint for Mac

1. Drag Spoilaint into the Applications folder.
2. Open it from Applications.

The first time, macOS says it can't check Spoilaint for malicious software (it isn't signed by an Apple
developer account yet). To open it anyway:

  - Click Done (or Cancel) on that message.
  - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to Spoilaint.
  - Confirm with your password or Touch ID. You only do this once.

On macOS 14 or older you can instead right-click Spoilaint in Applications, choose Open, then Open again.

Spoilaint then shows its setup window: click Install, then "Add to my browser" for Netflix, Prime, Hotstar
and YouTube. VLC needs to be installed for films on your Mac (videolan.org).

Along the way macOS may ask:
  - to let Spoilaint control Chrome, Brave or Edge: that's how it opens the extensions page for you.
  - to let it read files in Downloads, Documents or an external drive: that's where your films are; it
    listens to their dialogue to time the words.
