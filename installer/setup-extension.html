Spoilaint

Add Spoilaint to your browser

The easy way: pick your browser. Spoilaint opens its extensions page for you and puts the folder on your clipboard, with a small guide in the corner of the screen.

Chrome Brave Edge

If the browser asks whether to open Spoilaint, click Open. Nothing happens? Use the Start menu: Add Spoilaint to your browser.

Or by hand

  1. Type chrome://extensions in the address bar (Brave: brave://extensions, Edge: edge://extensions). Browsers don't let a page link there.
  2. Turn on Developer mode (switch at the top right).
  3. Copy the folder below, click Load unpacked, press Ctrl+V, then click Select Folder.
  4. Pin it: click the puzzle piece in the toolbar, then the pin next to Spoilaint.
